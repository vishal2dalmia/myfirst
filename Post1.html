We are all risk officers now

Vishal Dalmia

I've been thinking about AI and one idea keeps coming back to me.

Operating a machine used to be an art. Knowing which options to configure, and how to tweak them, took years of experience and domain knowledge. AI changes that. The same is true for setting up a campaign.

But something grows as a side effect: the need for effective controls and governance. When setup gets easier, someone has to spot what is risky or unintended.

In some sense, that makes all of us risk officers.
